# 📊 HR Data Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview
This HR analytics dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee demographics, training costs, job classifications, and departmental structures.  
It enables HR teams and management to monitor workforce distribution, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions related to staffing, training, and organizational development.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total employee count and workforce distribution.
- Visualize gender breakdown and marital status statistics.
- Highlight employee classifications (Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
- Explore department-level trends by gender and job title.
- Track organizational training cost and average employee age.

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- Power BI
- DAX Measures
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & ETL)
- Data Modeling (HR Schema)
- Pie charts, clustered bars, KPI visuals
- Slicers for interactive filtering (Gender, Employee Status, Department)

## 📁 Dataset
- **Total Employees:** 2,845  
- **Training Cost:** 1.59M  
- **Average Age:** 49.45  
- **Gender Ratio:** 55.82% Female / 44.18% Male  
- **Employee Types:** Full-Time, Part-Time, Temporary  
- **Departments Included:** Production, IT/IS, Sales, Software, Admin, Executive & more

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Female employees represent **55.82%**, indicating a strong gender balance tilt.
- Production department has the highest headcount.
- Most employees fall under **Temporary** and **Full-Time** roles.
- Training cost is significantly high (1.59M), suggesting active investment in employee development.
- Marital status distribution is balanced across categories (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed).
